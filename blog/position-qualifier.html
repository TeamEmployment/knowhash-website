New: Position Qualifier

Screen candidates before you ever pick up the phone

By Mike Reed, founder of knowhash

A recruiter's week is full of calls that end the same way: fifteen minutes in, and the basics aren't there. Not the wrong person, just the wrong moment to have found that out by phone.

The Position Qualifier fixes the order of operations. Pick a role, and knowhash writes a short, plain-English screening test in seconds — the kind of thing you'd want to know before the call, not during it. Send the link, and candidates answer it in their own time.

It's free to try right now, no account and nothing to install.

You can see the test generation for yourself here — pick a role, hit Generate, and watch it write a real screening test in front of you: knowhash.com/position-qualifier

Pick a role, get a real test

Choose from ten common roles and knowhash drafts the test on the spot — grouped sections, plain-English yes/no questions with a line explaining what each one is really asking, and one open question at the end for the recruiter to actually read.

Pick a role — the test is generated for that exact title.

See exactly what you're asking

Every question comes with the reasoning underneath it, so it never reads like a generic form. Skip what doesn't apply, answer what does.

Grouped by domain-specific vs. portable skills — not just a flat list of questions.

One email, and it's yours

The test itself is free to generate and read. To get a link you can actually send to candidates, drop in your email — that's it, no password, no account to set up.

Enter your email, and the real candidate link activates immediately.

Share it, then forget about it

You don't need to keep checking a dashboard. The moment a candidate completes it, their answers land straight in your inbox — question by question, ready to read over coffee.

Completed responses arrive by email — nothing to log back in and check.
