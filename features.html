Features

Everything a recruiter needs on LinkedIn — nothing hashed together.

knowhash Recruit sits alongside LinkedIn as one lightweight extension — from first contact to a finished shortlist package.

Candidate selection

Turn any LinkedIn contact into a candidate

The extension is lightweight and always available — park it to the side of any profile and add someone to your pipeline in one click, any time you're on LinkedIn.

Messaging

Build messages once, use them everywhere

This is where the name comes from — no need to hash about learning placeholders. A simple, low-friction editor lets you build templates from LinkedIn fields or your own preset values.

Shortlisting

Shortlist without leaving LinkedIn

Assign candidates to opportunities, add comments, mark suitability, and build shortlists as you go — no separate spreadsheet to keep in sync.

Data fulfilment

Ask for more, securely — with kSIP

Send a time-limited request when you need extra information or documents from a candidate. It's a lightweight system engineered to be secure and simple: nothing stored, nothing left behind.

See how kSIP works →
Sharing

Turn a profile into a shareable package

Produce a polished, branded package for a single candidate or a whole shortlist. What used to be a time-consuming write-up becomes a simple download.

Data minimization

Less data on your hands, less risk

Candidate data stays in your browser by default, minimizing your management responsibility, cost and exposure. Export it only when you need to — once you've narrowed things down to a shortlist.

kSIP, in three steps

A time-limited link goes out, the candidate unlocks it and sends their details straight back, and the link expires with nothing stored on any server.
